Our services

What we build.

From a single room to a whole new home — considered, well-built work across Tamworth and NSW, all on fixed-price contracts.

01

Bespoke new homes

Individually designed, hands-on-built family homes. We work with you from concept and approvals through to a finished home built to last.

02

Renovations & refurbishments

Full or partial renovations that modernise the way your home works while respecting what's already good about it.

03

Additions & alterations

Extensions, second storeys, reconfigured layouts and structural alterations that add space and value.

04

Heritage & federation homes

A genuine specialty: sensitive refurbishment of older and federation houses, preserving period character while upgrading comfort and safety.

05

Outdoor & patio spaces

Patios, decks and outdoor living areas built to match the home.

06

Approvals & building design

Help with building design, Council DAs and CDCs, permits and certifications — the whole approvals path handled as part of the job.
