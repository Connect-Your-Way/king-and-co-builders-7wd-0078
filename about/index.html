About King & Co

A builder who's on the tools.

King & Co Builders is a small Tamworth building firm led by David King, a licensed NSW builder with over 40 years' hands-on experience.

It's a deliberately boutique operation — David deals personally with every client, from the first in-home visit to the final handover, so the person quoting your job is the person building it.

The firm specialises in bespoke new homes, renovations and additions, and the careful refurbishment of older and federation houses across New South Wales.

Over four decades of hands-on building, brought to every King & Co project.
